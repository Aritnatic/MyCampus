Privacy policy

MyCampus is run for [COLLEGE NAME] ("the college") so students can find campus information and connect with each other. The college decides why and how your data is used (it is the "data fiduciary" / "controller"). Contact: [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL AND ADDRESS].

What we collect

WhatWhy

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or browsing history, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics trackers.

Who can see it

  • Verified students of your college can see your profile card (name, branch, year, skills, interests, bio) if you are on their campus. Your email is never shown to other students.
  • Students at other colleges can see your profile only if you switch on "Open to mentoring", and can see projects, startups and notes only if you mark them open to other colleges.
  • College admins can see the student list for their college (name, email, branch, semester, status), event registrations, safety-alert replies, and your replies to research listings. Admin actions are recorded in an audit log.
  • Notices, exams, results, faculty details, the campus map and safety alerts are visible only to verified members of your own college.
  • We never rank students against each other, and results shown in the app are about you only.

Who else handles your data

  • Hosting provider — [HOSTING PROVIDER, COUNTRY/REGION] stores the database and uploaded files.
  • Email provider — [Resend] delivers sign-up and password-reset codes. It sees your email address and the message.
  • Push notification services — if you allow notifications, Expo and Apple/Google deliver them to your phone.

We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Backups are kept for about 14 days, so deleted data can remain in a backup for up to that long. Safety-alert history and the admin audit log are kept for the college's records [RETENTION PERIOD — college to confirm].

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data — Me → Download my data.
  • Correct it — Me → Edit profile.
  • Delete your account — Me → Delete my account. This removes your profile, notes, requests and notifications, and signs you out everywhere. Projects you started with other members are handed to the next member.
  • Turn off notifications in your phone's settings, and choose whether to be discoverable as a mentor.

Security

Passwords are hashed; connections use HTTPS; access to each college's information is limited by your verified college email; sessions end when you change your password. No system is perfectly secure — if you think your account was accessed by someone else, change your password and tell [PRIVACY CONTACT].

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it applies.
